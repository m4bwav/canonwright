# Learnings: canonwright

Lessons that shaped [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) and cw.py. Seeded 2026-11-15 from a harvest of one owner's notes on several AI-video adaptations of novels (62 lessons; only the general ones are here, owner-specific ones live in the private sidecar). Harvest numbers in brackets. Entry shape: `L-NNN code-name · date · source · lesson · fix`.

- L-001 `read-the-book-cite-lines` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [1, 48] · Guidance from memory or film images drifts from the book. · Read the book through readwright; every canon line quotes and cites.
- L-002 `reread-before-ruling` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [2] · A re-read of the passage overturned a wrong morning note. · Check each visual claim against the passage before ruling.
- L-003 `rule-the-silent-traits` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [3] · A trait the book leaves out (hair length) drifts between shots. · State it as a ruling with its reason.
- L-004 `book-over-popular-image` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [4] · Film looks and fan art differ from the book. · Record which wins in `prefer`; the default is the book.
- L-005 `canon-face-test-first` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [5] · A batch of a known character drifted from its canon picture. · Show a face test beside the canon picture first.
- L-006 `edit-the-canon-picture` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [6, 58] · Text-to-image sculpts depth into a face canon draws flat. · Edit the canon picture at low denoise.
- L-007 `text-names-the-state` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [7, 53] · A small ref detail lost to the prompt words ("a scabbard" refilled an empty one). · Change bible text and refs together; text names the state.
- L-008 `never-say-misleading-names` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [8] · A canon name with an everyday meaning drew that thing. · `never_say` list, flagged by `cw.py check`.
- L-009 `animal-nouns-pull-heads` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [9] · Animal and skull nouns pulled whole heads. · Describe the shape.
- L-010 `wear-in-text-not-refs` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [10, 11] · A bloodied ref carried blood into shots before the wound. · Wear per beat in card text after checking the passage.
- L-011 `materials-by-making` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [12, 62] · Monument words drew dressed stone for a mud wall. · Describe how the material is built and weathers.
- L-012 `locked-corrections` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [13] · Later sessions undid earlier canon corrections. · `corrections` with `locked: true`; disagreements go to the owner.
- L-013 `audiobook-pronunciation` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [14, 57] · Guessed pronunciations were wrong; speech-to-text spelled invented names as everyday words. · Take names from the audiobook narrator.
- L-014 `hide-far-or-3d` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [15, 16, 22, 41] · Five text-to-image tries failed a canon weapon. · 3D model or guide, composited; or keep it a distant speck.
- L-015 `effect-ref-fires-twice` · 2026-11-15 · image-generation skill learnings [17] · A ref showing a fired beam made the model fire it again. · Start from the still before the effect.
- L-016 `counts-from-blockout` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [18, 32, 56] · Crowds lost counts, cloned faces, swapped sides; height words did not hold. · 3D blockout start image.
- L-017 `coded-checklist` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [19] · Canon misses were not countable until each was a coded yes/no claim; a local vision judge then agreed 84 % with human reads. · `checks` with code and ref; export to artwright.
- L-018 `invented-spectacle` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [46] · Models add spectacle the scene lacks. · A `no-...` check.
- L-019 `owner-ruling-beats-reference` · 2026-11-15 · film notes [60] · A reference photo showed parts the owner ruled out. · Record the owner ruling; it beats the picture.
