canonwright is a Claude Code plugin for adapting a published book with AI image and video models. It keeps a canon bible per book: what the text says each character, creature, place and object looks like, quoted with chapter refs; what you ruled where the book is silent; what the models keep getting wrong and what fixed it; and yes/no checks to score takes against.

Image and video models draw the popular picture of a story, not the book's. Frankenstein's creature comes out green with neck bolts; the 1818 text gives him yellow skin, flowing black hair and black lips. A bible makes the book's version the one every prompt and every review is held to.
